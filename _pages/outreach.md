Most of what happens in a neuroimaging lab never reaches the people whose taxes pay for it, or the patients and families whose lives the research is meant to change. I make short videos and posts that explain what brain imaging actually shows, what a stroke does to the brain, and how recovery works — in plain language, without the jargon.

If you are a patient, a caregiver, a student thinking about neuroscience, or just curious about what a brain scan can and cannot tell you, these are for you.

<h2>Talks for general audiences</h2>

I am happy to speak with schools, patient groups, and community organisations, in English or Spanish. Topics I am often asked about:

* What an MRI scan actually measures, and what it does not
* What happens in the brain during and after a stroke
* Why recovery is possible at all, and what rehabilitation is doing
* How animal research connects to treatments for people
* What a career in neuroscience research looks like day to day

Get in touch by email if you would like to arrange something.
