Macaque MRI does not work out of the box with tools built for human brains. This is an ongoing effort to build reproducible preprocessing pipelines for non-human primate structural, diffusion and resting-state data — field-of-view handling, template construction in NMT space, denoising, distortion correction and tractography — so that longitudinal analyses across dozens of animals stay consistent.
