title: "Repetitive head impacts and white matter integrity"
excerpt: "Longitudinal DTI and NODDI in collegiate football players and blast-exposed military personnel."
collection: portfolio
---

Head impacts that never rise to the level of a diagnosed concussion may still accumulate. Using longitudinal diffusion imaging — DTI and NODDI — in collegiate football players across a season, and in military personnel exposed to blast, this work asks whether white-matter microstructure changes are detectable before clinical symptoms appear.

Related work: [Repeated subconcussive head impacts compromise white matter integrity](/publication/2025-05-14-subconcussive-white-matter) (preprint) and [Longitudinal analysis of structural changes in blast-exposed military personnel](/publication/2025-05-01-blast-exposure-military), *Brain Injury* (2025).
