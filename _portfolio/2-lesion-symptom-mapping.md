Lesion-symptom mapping relates the spatial distribution of damage to behavioural outcome. My work in this area has compared multiple mapping approaches in the same stroke cohort to identify which spatial findings are robust across methods rather than artefacts of a single analysis choice.

Related publication: [Consistent spatial lesion-symptom patterns](/publication/2024-03-01-lesion-symptom-triangulation), *Magnetic Resonance Imaging* (2024).
