An ongoing longitudinal study of recovery after middle cerebral artery occlusion in macaques, combining structural, diffusion and resting-state MRI with repeated behavioural assessment. The goal is to identify imaging markers that track — and ideally predict — the trajectory of motor and cognitive recovery, and to evaluate how rehabilitation therapy modifies that trajectory.

*This project is in progress; results will be linked here once published.*
