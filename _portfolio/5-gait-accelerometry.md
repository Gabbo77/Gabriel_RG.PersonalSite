title: "Accelerometry-based gait quality analysis"
excerpt: "Quantifying dynamic gait stability from a single trunk-mounted inertial sensor."
collection: portfolio
---

A single accelerometer on the trunk can capture a surprising amount about how stable someone's walking is. This project develops and validates spatiotemporal indicators of dynamic gait stability in healthy adults, as a foundation for low-cost mobility assessment outside the gait lab.

Related publication: [Spatiotemporal indicators of dynamic gait stability using truncal accelerometry in healthy adults](/publication/2026-01-01-truncal-accelerometry-gait), *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* (2026).
